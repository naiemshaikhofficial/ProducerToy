**Standard Commercial Version:** 2.0 (Universal Multi-Vendor Standard)  
**Governing Standard:** Digital Audio Software, Soundware, and DSP Marketplace Distribution  

---

This Master Seller & Distribution Agreement (the **"Agreement"**) is entered into by and between:

1. **PRODUCERTOY** (referred to as **"ProducerToy"**, the **"Platform"**, or the **"Marketplace Operator"**), operating the digital marketplace at `producertoy.com` and developer portal at `creators.producertoy.com`; and
2. The independent software developer, sound design studio, DSP engineer, or publisher executing this Agreement or an accompanying Product Schedule (the **"Seller"**, **"Developer"**, or **"Brand"**).

ProducerToy and the Seller are individually referred to as a **"Party"** and collectively as the **"Parties"**.

## 1. PURPOSE & UNIVERSAL SCOPE

1.1 **Universal Multi-Vendor Model:** This Agreement establishes the standard commercial, legal, and operational terms governing the distribution, sale, and delivery of digital audio products through ProducerToy. This includes, without limitation:
- Audio effect and virtual instrument plugins (VST2, VST3, AU, AAX, CLAP, and Standalone applications).
- Sample packs, sound libraries, drum kits, and audio loops (WAV, AIFF, FLAC).
- Synthesizer presets, sound patches, and DSP expansion banks.
- DAW project templates, impulse responses (IRs), and digital soundware.

1.2 **Non-Exclusive Distribution:** The Seller appoints ProducerToy as a non-exclusive, worldwide digital distributor for the products approved in one or more executed Product Schedules (each, a **"Product Schedule"**). The Seller retains full freedom to distribute, license, and sell its products directly or through other third-party channels.

1.3 **Retention of Intellectual Property:** The Seller retains sole and exclusive ownership of all right, title, and interest—including all copyrights, patents, DSP algorithms, audio recordings, source code, and trademarks—in and to its products. ProducerToy acquires no ownership rights under this Agreement.

## 2. COMMERCIAL TERMS & SETTLEMENT ENGINE

2.1 **Revenue Share Allocation:**
- **Seller Share:** **70%** of Eligible Net Sale Proceeds.
- **ProducerToy Marketplace Commission:** **30%** of Eligible Net Sale Proceeds.

2.2 **Settlement Frequency & Payment Due Date:**
- Settlements are calculated on a calendar-month billing cycle (first to last day of each month).
- Payouts are due and disbursed by the **15th calendar day of the following month** (e.g., all January sales settle and disburse by February 15th).

2.3 **Zero Minimum Payout Threshold:**
- There is **no minimum payout threshold ($0.00)**. All cleared net balances due to the Seller are disbursed on the monthly schedule regardless of balance size.

2.4 **The 7-Step Net Settlement Deduction Hierarchy:**  
To guarantee deterministic, auditable accounting, the commission base and net payable royalty are calculated according to the following strict order:
1. **Customer Gross Price Paid:** Actual customer price collected after applying any valid promotional discounts.
   - *Seller-Funded Discounts:* If the Seller initiates or opts into a discount, the commission base is the discounted price.
   - *Platform-Funded Discounts:* If ProducerToy subsidizes a marketing campaign, the Seller's royalty is calculated on the full pre-discount base.
2. **Exclusion of Indirect Taxes:** Separately collected sales taxes, Goods and Services Tax (GST), or Value Added Tax (VAT) are legally excluded from the commission base.
3. **Deduction of Payment Processing & Conversion Fees:** Actual merchant acquiring fees (e.g., PayPal international merchant fee of 4.40% + $0.30, or Razorpay domestic rate) and transaction-specific currency conversion charges incurred at checkout are deducted to establish net realized funds.
4. **Reversal of Refunds & Chargebacks:** Net proceeds from refunded, cancelled, or successfully charged-back orders are reversed against the billing period.
5. **Eligible Net Sale Proceeds:** The resulting net cash amount establishes the official commission base.
6. **70/30 Net Split:**
   - 70% is credited to the Seller's ledger.
   - 30% is retained by ProducerToy for platform hosting, global CDN bandwidth, DSP telemetry, and marketplace administration.
7. **Outbound Transfer Fees:** Actual third-party bank wire or SWIFT transfer fees incurred to remit international payouts to the Seller's account are deducted without platform markup.

2.5 **Itemized Accounting Statements:** ProducerToy provides the Seller with a monthly digital accounting statement detailing units sold, gross customer collections, payment gateway fees, refunds, and net payable royalties, downloadable via CSV/PDF from the Toy Creator portal.

## 3. UNIVERSAL MULTI-VENDOR SUPPORT RESPONSIBILITY MODEL

To ensure transparent customer service across all participating brands, sound designers, and software manufacturers, customer support and operational responsibilities are allocated according to a universal multi-vendor division:

### 3.1 ProducerToy Platform Responsibilities
ProducerToy is solely responsible for marketplace-level infrastructure and customer account administration, including:
- Customer accounts, authentication, password recovery, and profile security.
- Checkout workflows, multi-currency processing, and transaction records.
- Order confirmation, tax invoice generation, and purchase history.
- ProducerToy customer library and high-speed global CDN download infrastructure.
- Investigating and remediating platform-side download, network, or delivery failures.
- Publisher commission accounting, monthly settlement ledgers, and disbursement statements.
- Platform bugs, security vulnerability reports, and marketplace policy enforcement.
- Coordinating customer refund and chargeback processing through configured payment gateways.

### 3.2 Seller / Brand Responsibilities
Each independent seller is solely responsible for the digital products and soundware it supplies, including:
- Accurate product titles, descriptions, technical specifications, and compatibility claims.
- Supported operating system versions, CPU architectures, DAW hosts, and plugin formats.
- Clear installation documentation, user manuals, and product-specific setup instructions.
- Product activation requirements, genuine license keys, serial numbers, and machine seat management.
- Audio software defects, DSP glitches, DAW crashes, plugin bugs, and product maintenance updates.
- Product-specific technical support and direct troubleshooting with end customers.
- Accuracy, originality, and legality of supplied installers, sample archives, presets, documentation, and artwork.
- Maintaining valid download packages, updated binary builds, release changelogs, and product-specific license terms.

### 3.3 Shared Responsibilities & Joint Cooperation
ProducerToy and the relevant seller shall cooperate in good faith on:
- Failed or incomplete digital delivery investigations.
- Missing, duplicated, invalid, or incorrectly assigned license keys (24–48 hour turnaround).
- Refund requests, fraud screening, and chargeback dispute evidence collection.
- Product security incidents, binary integrity verification, and suspected malware reports.
- Intellectual property notices and copyright ownership inquiries.
- Complex customer complaints that span both platform checkout and product software execution.
- Orderly product withdrawal, version deprecation, and ensuring ongoing library access for existing licensed buyers.

### 3.4 Cause-Based Allocation Principle
Operational liability, remediation duty, and costs are allocated strictly according to the root cause:
- **Platform-Side Failures:** ProducerToy investigates and resolves platform outages, payment gateway verification drops, order generation failures, and CDN delivery timeouts.
- **Product-Side Failures:** The Seller investigates and resolves defective binaries, corrupted sample archives, invalid supplied serial keys, activation server failures, and audio engine defects.
- **Dual-System Incidents:** Both parties coordinate when an issue involves both platform delivery and product software behavior.

## 4. PRODUCT LICENSING & ORDER OF PRECEDENCE

4.1 **Three-Tier Legal Precedence:** To resolve any conflict between platform rules and product licenses:
1. **Tier 1 (Platform Services):** ProducerToy's General Terms of Service, Marketplace EULA, and Purchase Policies govern marketplace accounts, checkout, billing, and platform delivery.
2. **Tier 2 (Software Operation):** The Seller's Product-Specific EULA or license text (set forth in the applicable Product Schedule) governs the execution, machine seats, DSP usage, soundware rights, and activation of that specific product.
3. **Tier 3 (Mandatory Law):** Non-waivable mandatory consumer protection or statutory laws supersede conflicting provisions in Tiers 1 and 2.

4.2 **Pre-Purchase Disclosure:** ProducerToy guarantees that the Seller's product-specific EULA, supported operating systems, formats, and seat restrictions will be linked and disclosed to the customer prior to order completion.

## 5. PRODUCT WITHDRAWAL & CONTINUED CUSTOMER ACCESS

5.1 **Orderly Discontinuation:** The Seller may discontinue or withdraw a product from future sale upon thirty (30) days' written notice to ProducerToy.

5.2 **Existing Customer Library Access:** To protect legitimate paying customers, ProducerToy retains a non-exclusive license to host and make available previously purchased product files in the libraries of existing purchasers, unless withdrawal is mandated by an urgent security defect or verified third-party copyright infringement claim.
