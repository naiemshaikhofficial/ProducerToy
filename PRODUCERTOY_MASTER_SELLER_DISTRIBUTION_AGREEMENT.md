## 5. CHECKOUT, PAYMENTS, ORDERS AND DELIVERY

1. **5.1 Server-Side Verified Checkout.** Orders are processed through Producer Toy checkout using authorized payment gateways with server-side webhook verification. No order or delivery is initiated on client-side status alone.
2. **5.2 Library and Protected Delivery.** Upon verified payment, the buyer receives access in their Producer Toy user library and access to the approved, secure, time-expiring or token-gated download path.
3. **5.3 Storage and Delivery Architecture.** Hosting and file distribution must follow Schedule C. External download links are not permitted unless their access control, link durability, and customer experience are tested and approved.
4. **5.4 License Activation Management.** For products requiring serial numbers or activation:
   - **Method A (CSV Key Pool):** Seller supplies pre-generated genuine serial keys via Toy Creator. Keys are assigned uniquely (1 key per customer purchase) with zero duplicate issuance.
   - **Method B (Live Webhook API):** Platform dispatches a cryptographically signed (HMAC-SHA256) server-to-server HTTP POST request to Seller's keygen endpoint with a 3,000ms SLA, falling back to emergency CSV pool if unreachable.
   - **Method C (Direct / Unlocked):** Product utilizes hardware machine-locked challenge/response or royalty-free zip distribution without serial keys.
5. **5.5 Failed Fulfilment Response.** If delivery or activation fails, Producer Toy diagnoses checkout, account, and download infrastructure; Seller diagnoses key validity, activation servers, and build defects. Both Parties cooperate to resolve mixed issues within 24–48 hours.
6. **5.6 Third-Party Infrastructure.** Producer Toy utilizes tier-1 payment gateways, cloud hosting (Supabase, AWS/Cloudflare CDN), and email delivery. While using reasonable efforts to maintain uptime, Producer Toy does not guarantee uninterrupted third-party service operation.
7. **5.7 Merchant Role and Customer Invoicing.** The Parties settle transaction roles (merchant-of-record vs. marketplace intermediary) and invoicing responsibility prior to public launch. Neither Party will be styled as merchant-of-record or reseller unless the checkout architecture and tax review support that designation.
8. **5.8 Excluded Transactions.** Revenue share is payable only on eligible amounts actually collected. Fraudulent, duplicate, test, chargeback, reversed, or refunded transactions do not generate payable revenue share.

## 8. REVENUE SHARE, DEDUCTIONS AND STATEMENTS

1. **8.1 Standard Revenue Split.** The standard revenue share is **70% to Seller** and **30% to Producer Toy**, calculated on **Eligible Net Proceeds**.
2. **8.2 Eligible Net Proceeds Defined.**
   $$\text{Eligible Net Proceeds} = \text{Gross Order Receipts} - (\text{Discounts} + \text{Transaction Taxes} + \text{Actual Payment Processing Fees} + \text{Refunds/Chargebacks} + \text{FX Conversion Costs})$$
   No undisclosed platform markups or hidden fees may be deducted.
3. **8.3 Discounts & Promotional Pricing.** Discounts authorized by Seller reduce the customer purchase price used in calculations. Discounts funded unilaterally by Producer Toy do not reduce Seller's revenue share unless agreed in writing.
4. **8.4 Transparent Itemized Statements.** Monthly statements in Toy Creator provide transparent breakdowns by order ID, currency, processing fee, and net split. Currencies (e.g., USD and INR) are kept strictly separate and never summed without clear FX conversion records.
5. **8.5 Illustrative Calculation ($19.00 Standard Product):**
   - Customer Price: **$19.00**
   - Actual Gateway Processing Fee (~6% illustrative): **-$1.14**
   - Eligible Net Proceeds: **$17.86**
   - **Seller Share (70%): $12.50**
   - **Producer Toy Share (30%): $5.36**  
   *(Actual processor fees governed by real gateway receipts; international payout rail fees addressed under Section 9).*
