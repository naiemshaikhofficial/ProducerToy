# PRODUCERTOY CUSTOMER SUPPORT & MULTI-VENDOR RESOLUTION POLICY
**Effective Date:** October 2026  
**Applies To:** All Registered Customers, Audio Producers, and Marketplace Buyers  
**Governing Standard:** Universal Multi-Vendor Support Responsibility Model  

---

## 1. PURPOSE & SUPPORT OVERVIEW

ProducerToy is a curated digital marketplace hosting software plugins, virtual instruments, sample packs, presets, and sound design tools developed by independent audio software manufacturers, DSP engineers, and sound design studios worldwide.

To ensure fast, effective resolution of customer inquiries, support responsibilities are clearly allocated between **ProducerToy (the Marketplace Platform)** and the **Independent Seller / Audio Brand (the Product Manufacturer)**.

---

## 3. PRODUCERTOY PLATFORM RESPONSIBILITIES

ProducerToy operates the marketplace infrastructure and directly resolves:

1. **Customer Accounts & Security:**
   - Account creation, two-factor authentication, email updates, and password resets.
2. **Checkout, Payments & Invoices:**
   - Payment authorization issues, declined cards, UPI timeouts, and PayPal checkouts.
   - Formal GST/VAT tax invoices, purchase receipts, and transaction reference numbers.
3. **Customer Library & Download Infrastructure:**
   - Access to your persistent digital library at `producertoy.com/library`.
   - Resolving broken download mirrors, network timeouts, or slow CDN bandwidth.
4. **Order Status & Fulfillment Coordination:**
   - Verifying that purchased products and associated license records appear in your account immediately after payment.
5. **Refund & Chargeback Administration:**
   - Formal intake, review, and gateway disbursement of approved refunds and chargeback inquiries in accordance with the [Refund Policy](file:///d:/Website/ProducerToy/src/app/refund-policy/page.tsx).

---

## 4. INDEPENDENT SELLER / BRAND RESPONSIBILITIES

Each software brand or sound designer is the creator of its products and directly resolves:

1. **Installation & Operating System Setup:**
   - Step-by-step guidance for macOS (Apple Silicon native & Intel) and Windows installations.
   - Operating system permissions, Apple Gatekeeper notarization, or Windows Defender notices.
2. **DAW Host Compatibility:**
   - Assisting with plugin rescans and validation in FL Studio, Ableton Live, Logic Pro, Pro Tools, Studio One, Reaper, Cubase, and Bitwig Studio.
3. **Product Activation & License Management:**
   - Validating serial keys, challenge-response machine activations, and seat resets.
4. **Audio Engine Bugs & DSP Glitches:**
   - Investigating audio dropouts, distortion, CPU spikes, sample rate mismatches, or crash reports.
   - Releasing maintenance patches, bug fixes, and version updates.
5. **Soundware Content & Documentation:**
   - Inquiries regarding preset banks, sample mapping, MIDI loops, and sound design documentation.

---

## 5. JOINT COOPERATION PROTOCOL

When an issue involves both the platform purchase and the product software, ProducerToy and the Seller work in direct coordination:

1. **Missing or Invalid License Keys:**
   - If a license key fails to activate, ProducerToy verifies the transaction and coordinates with the brand's engineering team to issue a validated replacement key within **24 to 48 hours**.
2. **Defective Products & Incompatibility:**
   - If a customer experiences an irreconcilable software defect confirmed by the brand's technical support, ProducerToy authorizes and executes an immediate refund to the original payment source.
3. **Product Discontinuation:**
   - If a seller discontinues a product, ProducerToy maintains ongoing secure download access in the personal libraries of customers who previously purchased the license.

---

## 6. SUPPORT SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENTS (SLAS)

- **Initial Response:** All inquiries submitted to `support@producertoy.com` or through the Help Desk are acknowledged within **24 hours**.
- **Resolution Target:** Technical inquiries and transaction verifications are targeted for complete resolution within **D+4 business days** (where 'D' is the date of inquiry receipt).
- **Escalation Desk:** If your inquiry requires manufacturer intervention and the brand has not responded within 48 hours, ProducerToy's senior operations team will step in to mediate or process an authorized resolution.
